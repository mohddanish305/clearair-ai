    Method & sources
    
    
      
      
    • Data: Open-Meteo (Copernicus CAMS), free, no key — a modelled grid estimate for your area, not a CPCB station reading.
      • 
      
    • India NAQI computed here from CPCB breakpoints (24-h mean). US AQI from the same feed — the scales differ, so both are shown.
      • 
      
    • WHO: PM2.5 vs the WHO 2021 24-h guideline, 15 µg/m³.
      • 
      
    • Cigarette equivalent: Berkeley Earth rule — 24-h PM2.5 of 22 µg/m³ ≈ 1 cigarette/day. An illustration, not a medical measure.
      • 
      
    • Fixed rules, not AI. Verdicts & risk scores come from deterministic rules. AI only words them — it never sets a verdict or invents a number.
      • 
      
    • Impact figures (CO₂, exposure) are estimates from your answers — labelled as estimates.
      • 
    
    
    
    Sources: Open-Meteo / CAMS air quality · CPCB (India NAQI) · WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines 2021 · Berkeley Earth — cigarette equivalence
    
    
    No pollen for India — the live feed covers Europe only; no invented numbers.
